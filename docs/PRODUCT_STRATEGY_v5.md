## 1. Executive Summary & Core Philosophy

Neuroviax AI combines core operational software with **6 domain-scoped AI assistants** that generate actionable, explainable recommendations across sales, procurement, finance, and operations.

### The Paradigm Shift: ERP vs. ABOP
Traditional ERP tools operate on a retrospective model:
$$\text{Record} \longrightarrow \text{Report} \longrightarrow \text{Human Decides}$$

Neuroviax ABOP reframes the operating system:
$$\text{Record} \longrightarrow \text{AI Analyzes} \longrightarrow \text{AI Recommends} \longrightarrow \text{Human Approves} \longrightarrow \text{Workflow Executes}$$

## 4. The 6 Domain-Scoped AI Assistants

Rather than one monolithic general-purpose chatbot, Neuroviax employs 6 constrained copilots:

1. **Sales Assistant**: Demand forecasting, customer segmentation, next-best-offer.
2. **Procurement Assistant**: Reorder proposals, supplier price trend comparisons, cost optimization.
3. **Inventory Assistant**: Real-time stock alerts, shrinkage anomaly detection, reorder timing.
4. **Marketing Assistant**: Targeted promotional segments, loyalty reward recommendations.
5. **Customer Support Assistant**: Automated WhatsApp draft replies, order tracking, escalation flags.
6. **Finance Assistant**: 30-day cash-flow forecasting, expense categorization, ledger reconciliation.

---

## 5. Risk-Tiered Autonomy Model

Autonomy is scoped and safe. High-impact operations never bypass human verification:

- **Low Risk (Confidence > 0.85)**: Flag low stock, draft reorder suggestions $\rightarrow$ *Fully autonomous, notify only*.
- **Medium Risk**: Auto-send payment reminders, apply pre-approved discount tiers $\rightarrow$ *Autonomous within configured bounds*.
- **High Risk**: Place purchase order above threshold, issue refunds $\rightarrow$ *Requires explicit one-tap human approval*.
